It will depend on what you do to navigate yourself to break into the market. It's worth while to have an engineering path alongside your CS degree. Look at companies offering internships or associate programs - that way you can involve yourself into real world projects and obtain experience.

as for jobs, it's not about signing up online and hoping for a call back - even though that still works for many. Today, it's about networking, coffee chats, engagement and portfolio improvement and finding a mentor (if possible).

if you can build a website now that you can invest in as you develop over the next few years, that will separate you from the rest of the market. Employers want to know why they should invest in you.
