CCNY CS is hyped up because it's in the engineering school, and CCNY is the only CUNY with an engineering school.

Hunter is hyped up because it's the most popular CUNY school/the one with the most students, so Hunter students tend to be well-represented. I'm at Hunter, and I'm planning on transferring to Buffalo since there's a heavy liberal arts emphasis here (taking 4 foreign languages, a bunch of diversity classes, etc.)

I think Queens has the best CS program. Brooklyn is decent, John Jay is good for cybersecurity, and although Baruch doesn't have CS, I've heard good things about their Financial Math program which is somewhat of a CS and Finance/Business hybrid.

In general, I think CUNY schools are mediocre for CS since the courses are outdated.
