CUNY SPS - Master’s in Data Science
I am currently working towards Master’s in Data Science at CUNY SPS. It is a challenging program if you have no prior coding experience or work experience. If you are, it is not that hard just time consuming. There are a lot of homework and study sessions as well as extra readings and meetups. The department works closely with NYU Data Science departments and they are holding monthly meetups. If you want to learn - there are plenty of knowledgeable people and resources.

But as it is an online degree, for those who just want to get masters while working full time it is definitely worth considering (if you are already in SWE or related fields). It is hard to take more than 2 classes per semester due to the workload (if you have a full time job). If you are looking into this program without any coding experience, education etc hoping to get into the tech field - I think you need to consider in person programs. (Just my take here, as an in person CS major - I know what I am talking about)

This program is fantastic for people who are already in the tech field or want to continue CS/Math/Statistics academic education while searching for a SWE or Data Analytics or related job!
